How it works

Ten minutes. Three moves.

Open the list

We scan X for posts that are about to take off in your niche: founders, dev tools, AI, marketing. You get 5–8 posts with a one-line brief on what to say. No timeline scrolling.

02 · REPLY, DON'T LURK

Questions

Before you ask

Is this automation? Will it post for me?

No. Nothing touches your account. You get a list and a brief; you write the reply. Automated replies get accounts suspended and everyone can tell they're bots. The 10 minutes are yours.

Why replies and not my own posts?

A new account's posts reach its followers, which is nobody. A reply under a post with 200k views is shown to people already reading. In our dataset the reply-driven posts got 75 replies per 100 likes; that's where the follows come from.

What's in the daily list exactly?

5 to 8 posts, each with: who posted, why it will grow (velocity, author size, format), a one-line brief for your reply, and how long the window is open. Posted every morning European time, updated at noon.

I'm not a founder. Is it for me?

Yes if you sell to founders, developers or marketers, or want to be seen by them. Designers, devrel, agency owners and freelancers use the same lists.

How does the €499 plan work?

Week one: a 45-minute voice interview and a look at your product. Then you get 30 posts for the month in a shared doc, in batches, edited with you. You post them or schedule them. We track what worked and adjust next month.

Invoices, VAT, cancelling?

Stripe handles payment, invoices and EU VAT. You can cancel from the link in your receipt email; access stays until the end of the paid month. Business customers can add a VAT ID at checkout.
